Taste as Context

The less context you provide an AI, the more its responses regress to the mean.

Your taste - your unique combination of aesthetic preferences that are tough to duplicate - is leverage in the world where everyone can build with AI.

The Taste app records your observations about good and bad taste, stores it in a structured manner, and feeds it to your AI tools via MCP so everything you produce is informed by your taste.
